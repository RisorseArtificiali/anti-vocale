# Play Store Listing - Anti-Vocale

## Short Description (80 chars max)
```
Turn voice messages into text. Works offline, right on your phone.
```

Too many voice notes and no time to listen? Turn them into text and read them whenever you want.

Anti-Vocale turns the voice messages of WhatsApp, Telegram, and any other app into text, directly on your phone. It works on recorded calls and audio files too. Everything happens on your device: the audio never leaves your phone.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Long-press a voice message and share it with Anti-Vocale
2. Read the transcript that arrives in the notification
3. Copy it or send it back to the chat with one tap

WORKS IN YOUR LANGUAGE
- Italian, English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish and 20+ more European languages, right after install
- Hindi, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Turkish and Russian included
- Real-time mode: words appear while they are spoken, in 45 languages
- Russian, Persian, Hebrew, Ukrainian and more through free community models that install in two taps
- Missing yours? Browse the built-in catalog and add it

MORE THAN JUST TEXT
- AI summaries: long transcripts become a short, readable recap
- Who said what: conversations get speaker labels so you can follow along
- Any length works: an hour-long meeting becomes a document, not a problem
- Export as subtitle files or timestamped text for videos

EVERYDAY COMFORTS
- Read without opening the app: long transcripts page through right in the notification, with one-tap copy and send-back
- Find anything again: full-text search across everything you've transcribed
- Listen again: replay the original audio from any past result, with speed control
- Automatic: new WhatsApp voice notes become transcripts by themselves, with the sender's name
- Set it and forget it: auto-copy to clipboard or save to a folder you pick
- Video files too: just share or pick any video and get the words out

BUILT TO KEEP WORKING
- No internet needed after the first model download
- Survives aggressive battery savers (common on Realme, Oppo, OnePlus, Vivo)
- Recovers on its own from crashes and corrupted downloads

PRIVACY, FOR REAL
No account, no ads, no tracking, no data collection. Your audio stays on your phone, always.

OPEN SOURCE
Free and open source. Want the technical details, the model list, measured quality per language, and how to contribute? Everything is on GitHub: https://github.com/RisorseArtificiali/anti-vocale
