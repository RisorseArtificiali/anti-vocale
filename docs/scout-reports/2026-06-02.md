## ASR Model Findings

### ilmina/qwen3-asr-1.7b-sherpa-onnx (NEW)
- **Source:** https://huggingface.co/ilmina/qwen3-asr-1.7b-sherpa-onnx
- **Size:** ~2.4 GB (encoder.int8.onnx + decoder.int8.onnx + conv_frontend.onnx)
- **Languages:** 52 languages including Italian (Qwen3-ASR family)
- **Last updated:** 2026-05-17
- **Format:** ONNX int8, native sherpa-onnx format
- **sherpa-onnx compatible:** **Yes** — native sherpa-onnx file structure (encoder + decoder + conv_frontend)
- **Italian quality assessment:** Unbenchmarkied for Italian. The 0.6B Qwen3-ASR scored 12.2% WER in our 4-model benchmark vs 4.3% for Distil IT and 5.4% for Parakeet. The 1.7B variant should be significantly better but is 5x the size of Parakeet (2.4 GB vs 464 MB). The size-quality tradeoff needs measurement.
- **Score:** 6.0/10 (Italian quality: 5 — unproven at 1.7B, Size: 4 — 2.4 GB is large for ASR, On-device: 8 — native sherpa-onnx, Multilingual: 8 — 52 langs, Maintenance: 7 — community conversion)
- **Recommendation:** **evaluate** — first 1.7B Qwen3-ASR in sherpa-onnx format. Worth benchmarking Italian WER against Parakeet and Distil IT. The 2.4 GB size is the main concern; if Italian WER doesn't beat Parakeet at 464 MB, it's not worth the size.
- **Effort to integrate:** low — same sherpa-onnx pipeline, just new model download URL and config
- **Notes:** Alternative to csukuangfj's official 0.6B conversion. Same base model (Qwen3-ASR-1.7B), different converter. Apache-2.0 license.

### Mega-ASR / zhifeixie/Mega-ASR (NEW)
- **Source:** https://huggingface.co/zhifeixie/Mega-ASR
- **GGUF variant:** https://huggingface.co/cstr/mega-asr-GGUF (407 downloads, tags include `it`)
- **LiteRT variant:** https://huggingface.co/Reza2kn/mega-asr-litert (51 downloads)
- **Size:** Qwen3-ASR-1.7B base; LiteRT files: `mega-asr-llm_q4_block32_ekv512.tflite` + `audio_encoder_fp32.onnx`
- **Languages:** GGUF claims 30+ languages including Italian; LiteRT only lists en/zh/ja/ko
- **Last updated:** 2026-05-27 (safetensors), 2026-05-22 (GGUF/LiteRT)
- **Format:** safetensors (source), GGUF (cstr), TFLite/LiteRT (Reza2kn)
- **sherpa-onnx compatible:** No — GGUF or LiteRT formats, not ONNX
- **Italian quality assessment:** Unconfirmed. The GGUF variant tags Italian but the LiteRT variant doesn't. The "robust ASR" training (far-field, echo, dropout, noise, distortion) is directly relevant to voice message transcription. Quality for Italian is unknown.
- **Score:** 5.4/10 (Italian quality: 4 — unconfirmed, Size: 5 — 1.7B base, On-device: 7 — LiteRT exists, Multilingual: 7 — 30+ langs claimed, Maintenance: 6 — research model)
- **Recommendation:** **watch** — the "robust ASR" focus is highly relevant to our voice message use case. If Italian quality is confirmed and the LiteRT variant works with Italian, this could be a valuable addition. Needs testing.
- **Effort to integrate:** medium — would need LiteRT pipeline (not sherpa-onnx), and Italian language support verification
- **Notes:** The audio_encoder_fp32.onnx + TFLite LLM split is similar to the Gemma 4 LiteRT pattern. The "robust" training data (noisy, far-field) addresses a real gap in our current models.

## Landscape Developments

**Nemotron streaming in sherpa-onnx (English)** validates the full streaming ASR architecture for mobile devices. The documentation confirms 4 chunk sizes (80ms–1120ms) and real-time streaming inference. The arXiv paper 2604.14493 ("Pushing the Limits of On-Device Streaming ASR") shows Nemotron int4 k-quant at 0.67 GB achieving 8.2% streaming WER — a new Pareto-optimal point. If the multilingual variant gets NVIDIA permission, we could have streaming Italian ASR at a similar size point.

**Mega-ASR** introduces "robust ASR" as a training objective — specifically targeting noisy, far-field, echo-distorted, and dropout-affected audio. This is directly relevant to voice message transcription where audio quality varies significantly. The LiteRT variant (TFLite + ONNX audio encoder) is an Android-native deployment format, though Italian language support needs verification.

**IBM Granite 4.0 1B Speech** is a new compact speech model at 1B params with keyword list biasing (useful for domain vocabulary). Covers EN/FR/DE/ES/PT/JA — **no Italian**. Apache 2.0. Worth watching for Italian support in future versions.

**Gladia's open-source STT benchmark** ranks models: Canary-Qwen 2.5B (5.63% WER) > Granite Speech 3.3 8B > Qwen3-ASR > Whisper Large V3 (7.4%) > Parakeet TDT (~8.0%). Important caveat: "benchmark WER and production performance diverge significantly for conversational audio." This aligns with our own finding that Parakeet performs better on real WhatsApp audio than synthetic benchmarks suggest.
