## Headline findings

### 1. VibeVoice-ASR-Streaming 1.5B on litert-community (composite 8.2/10)

litert-community/VibeVoice-ASR-Streaming-1.5B (published 2026-09-27,
MIT, base microsoft/VibeVoice-ASR-Streaming-1.5B).

- What it is: streaming, SPEAKER-ATTRIBUTED ASR (transcription and
  diarization in one pass; interleaved audio chunks + lookahead + prior
  text). 10 languages: en zh es pt de ja ko fr ru it.
- Why it matters here: it rides litert-lm, the SAME runtime our Gemma
  backend already ships (LlmTranscriptionBackend). The .litertlm
  artifact is 1.99 GB fp16 / 2.77 GB int8 (the int8 is LARGER, unusual
  packaging). Speaker attribution would replace our separate diarization
  pass for its languages, and streaming gives progressive display free.
- Scores: quality 4 (arXiv 2609.02812: best/tied speaker attribution on
  12/13 settings; no Italian-specific WER yet = unverified for it, but
  Italian is in the model's language list), size 3 (2-2.8 GB, within our
  LLM lane ceiling), feasibility 5 (the runtime is already shipped),
  coverage 4 (it/ru/pt add depth), catalog-import 1 (NOT sherpa: it
  would be a second engine on the LLM lane, a Gemma sibling, not an
  external family), maintenance 4 (Microsoft + litert-community, days
  old).
- Path: not a community-catalog entry; this is a LlmTranscriptionBackend
  sibling (a new .litertlm model behind the existing lane) or a successor
  to Gemma-ASR if it proves out. Probe first: does our LiteRT-LM version
  load the artifact, and what does the Italian quality look like on the
  11-clip set (input-chain labeled).

### 2. VibeVoice-ASR (non-streaming) community ONNX export (composite 6.5/10)

christopherthompson81/vibevoice-asr-onnx: ONNX export of
microsoft/VibeVoice-ASR-HF (audio_encoder + decoder_single + tokenizer,
documented graph contract, KV-cache parity check). Not in sherpa layout
and 7B-class; sherpa has no VibeVoice family. Watch only: if sherpa ever
adds a family, this export is proven.

### 3. Nemotron 3.5 streaming: community validation of our choice (no action)

The HF discussion on nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-ASR-Streaming-Multilingual-0.6b
has onnx-export usage reports with strong code-switch/accent handling
("the German-accented speaker's Swedish comes out word-perfect"). We
already ship this engine (1120ms int8); third-party confirmation.

### 4. nabra-82m Arabic int8 (composite 5.9/10)

csukuangfj/nabra-82m-arabic-int8 (2026-09-08; the newest ASR in the
csukuangfj firehose this month, the rest is TTS: kokoro, vits, matcha).
82M params, Arabic, sherpa-native export. Our Arabic coverage today is
whisper-arabic-dialectal (~1 GB); an 82M-class model would be the
light-Arabic row. Unverified quality (no published WER found); fits the
Transducer or CTC family per config (needs the family probe). Community
catalog candidate after a device validation pass.

### 5. Fun-ASR-Nano-2512 on litert-community (composite 5.6/10)

litert-community/Fun-ASR-Nano-2512 (2026-09-27, Apache-2.0, FunAudioLLM

already covered better (SenseVoice zh/ja, whisper en); no Italian.
Watch list.
