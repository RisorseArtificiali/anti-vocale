## Executive Summary

Two findings outrank everything else, and the second is not a model at all. First, `doanhlt/cabin-asr-eu-parakeet-tdt-offline-r1` (published today) is a fr/de/es/it fine-tune of the exact Parakeet TDT v3 we ship, exported in our exact sherpa int8 package shape, claiming Italian FLEURS 4.03 to 2.02 against the shipped package, with paired bootstraps and fp32 references. ### int8 packaging defect in the shipped Parakeet v3 stock package (not a model; an action)
- **Source:** measured and published in the cabin-asr card above (`eval/block_sensitivity*.json`, `export/excl_pre+dw.json`)
- **Diagnosis:** sherpa's `quantize_dynamic` per-tensor recipe quantizes every MatMul AND Conv. In conformer depthwise convs, folded BatchNorm leaves a few channels with |w| near 100 to 240 (median channel max |w| is 0.27), so one per-tensor uint8 scale zeroes out 54 to 93 percent of the channels in layers 1, 2, and 22; the five 8x-subsampling `pre_encode` convs are the second fragile group. The defect exists in NVIDIA's own shipped v3 int8 package: 0.8 to 2.1 FLEURS points lost against the same fp32 weights. Fine-tuning re-estimates BatchNorm, so any re-export of a fine-tune loses a lottery amount
- **The fix:** identical recipe with `nodes_to_exclude` on the 29 fragile Conv nodes (5 pre_encode + 24 depthwise, under 0.1 percent of weights, kept fp32); size within 1 MiB of the shipped package; the fp32 convs are also FASTER than ConvInteger on x86. Their block-sensitivity table shows all-but-pre+dw quantization moves the encoder output 2.5x less than full quantization, and WER equals fp32
- **Score:** not scored (an action on our own artifact)
- **Recommendation:** re-export the stock v3 with this exclusion list and A/B it in eval/ against the shipped package; separately A/B our smoothquant default, whose recipe differs and may or may not share the defect (unmeasured). This also retroactively explains part of why our harness sees ~4.3 it FLEURS for a model whose fp32-class reference rows sit near 2.6 to 1.8
- **Effort:** low (the exclusion list and export script are published in the repo)
- **Notes:** converges with the #4127 quantization lesson from 2026-10-28 (per-channel encoder-only int8 there, exclusion-list here; same principle: the depthwise/subsampling convs are the fragile nodes). Any conversion we adopt or produce from here on (including abid1010's parakeet-ultra int8 from yesterday) should be checked against this recipe

## Landscape Developments

The conversion ecosystem has fully decentralized. This week's phone-relevant artifacts came from five different independent converters (abid1010, solavr, diarizeapp, doanhlt, Kavic) plus the litert-community org, not from the model authors; the firehose account (csukuangfj) is stale. And the sharpest technical lesson of the week consolidates: quantization recipe quality now outweighs checkpoint choice at the 0.6B tier. Three independent reports in three days (sherpa #4127's 2.9-WER recipe lesson, yesterday's, and today's cabin block-sensitivity analysis showing the STOCK package we ship loses up to 2.1 points to its own fp32) all land on the same nodes: depthwise and subsampling convs under per-tensor dynamic quantization. Every future adoption decision should treat the export recipe as part of the model identity, not packaging detail.

## Same-Day Mac Validation (TASK-922)

The maintainer ordered same-day Mac validation of the top findings; the full evidence lives in TASK-922 and `eval/results/fleurs100*_20261029.json`. Both top recommendations CONFIRMED in our stack (sherpa-onnx 2.0.1, fleurs_bench.py, 100 clips per language, 12 tradition languages):

- The stock int8 defect is real: shipped 640MB package loses to fp32 on every language measured (it 4.62 vs 3.06, fr 8.88 vs 6.23, de 8.04 vs 4.80, es 4.01 vs 3.26), and the exclusion-fix re-export of the same weights lands within 0.04 to 0.55 of fp32 everywhere at unchanged size and unchanged Mac RTF. On the extended 8-language sweep the fix improves every language (nl 11.73 to 9.18, pl 13.40 to 8.81, fi 19.61 to 14.18, el 47.73 to 38.90, uk 9.99 to 7.98, ru 8.23 to 6.08, en 6.88 to 5.42, pt-BR 6.84 to 5.65).
- cabin-asr-eu adds real fine-tune gains on top of fp32 (fr 5.96, de 4.59, it 2.98, es 3.26), confirming the fr/de/es catalog case.
- The twist: on our 11 real Italian voice clips the shipped int8 BEATS its own fp32 (0.041 vs 0.059 WER); the zeroed-channel defect behaves as an accidental denoiser on messy WhatsApp-class audio. The fixed packages restore true fp32 behavior there (0.060/0.062). Domain-dependent, measured, not a recipe bug.
- abid1010's parakeet-ultra export fails to load in sherpa at all (encoder missing `vocab_size` metadata); that specific export is unusable as-is.
