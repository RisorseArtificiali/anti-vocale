# Model Scout Report — 2026-06-05

## Executive Summary

Short cycle (1 day since last report). **Nemotron-3.5-ASR Streaming Multilingual has officially released** (June 4, 2026) — 0.6B params, 40 languages with Italian in the top transcription-ready tier, streaming capable, and community ONNX exports already available (FP16 ~1.25 GB, INT8 ~720 MB). This is the most consequential model release for Anti-Vocale since Parakeet TDT v3 integration. Also: **LiteRT-LM v0.13.1 released** (upgrade from our v0.12.0), Gemma 4 QAT Mobile GGUF variants appeared with "any-to-any" pipeline tags, and a new **ARK-ASR-0.6B** model distilled from Qwen3-ASR emerged. sherpa-onnx v1.13.2 remains current at 23 days — no new release yet, though ORT override and QNN streaming PRs are merged and accumulating.

## ASR Model Findings

### Nemotron-3.5-ASR Streaming Multilingual — OFFICIALLY RELEASED (CRITICAL)
- **Source:** https://huggingface.co/nvidia/nemotron-3.5-asr-streaming-0.6b
- **Discussion:** https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-ASR-Streaming-Multilingual-0.6b/discussions/1
- **Architecture:** Cache-Aware FastConformer-RNNT, 600M params, 24 encoder layers
- **License:** OpenMDW-1.1 (commercial use allowed)
- **Size:** 0.6B params
- **Languages (40 total, 3 tiers):**
  - **Transcription-ready (19):** en, es, fr, **it**, pt, nl, de, tr, ru, ar, hi, ja, ko, vi, uk — plus locale variants
  - **Broad-coverage (13):** pl, sv, cs, no, da, bg, fi, hr, sk, zh, hu, ro, et
  - **Adaptation-ready (8):** requires fine-tuning
- **Streaming:** Configurable chunks: 80ms, 160ms, **320ms**, 560ms, 1120ms
- **Italian performance:** 5.23 WER (LangID), 4.85 WER (auto-detect) on FLEURS at 1.12s chunk
- **Auto language detection:** `target_lang=auto` mode detects language and tags output
- **Built-in punctuation and capitalization**
- **Downloads:** 597 in first day; 184 likes
- **sherpa-onnx compatible:** Not yet — English-only Nemotron streaming is integrated; multilingual variant not yet added. csukuangfj requested export permission (May 20 discussion). Model is now public under OpenMDW license.
- **Community ONNX exports already available:**

| ONNX FP16 | `soniqo/Nemotron-3.5-ASR-Streaming-Multilingual-0.6B-ONNX-FP16` | ~1.25 GB | Best quality |

- **Android SDK:** soniqo provides `speech-android` (https://github.com/soniqo/speech-android) with cache management, streaming, and RNN-T greedy decoding
- **Fine-tuning:** NVIDIA published HuggingFace blog post "How to Fine-Tune Nemotron 3.5 ASR for Your Language, Domain, or Accent" — confirmed Italian fine-tuning possible
- **Score:** 9.5/10 (Italian quality: 8 — comparable to Parakeet with streaming, Size: 8 — 0.6B same class as Parakeet, On-device: 9 — ONNX/LiteRT exports exist + Android SDK, Multilingual: 10 — 40 langs, Maintenance: 10 — NVIDIA official + community exports)
- **Recommendation:** **HIGHEST PRIORITY — EVALUATE IMMEDIATELY** — (1) test Italian WER of ONNX FP16 vs current Parakeet and Distil IT, (2) verify sherpa-onnx integration path or evaluate soniqo SDK, (3) benchmark INT8 build Italian quality specifically, (4) evaluate streaming for progressive display
- **Effort to integrate:** medium — either wait for sherpa-onnx integration or use soniqo SDK; streaming requires managing encoder caches (attention, conv, pre-cache)

The FP16 at ~1.25 GB is larger than Parakeet int8 (464 MB) but smaller than Whisper Distil IT (938 MB). The auto language detection feature could simplify the user experience significantly.

- **Size:** 0.6B params
- **Languages:** Multilingual including Italian
- **Last updated:** May 27, 2026
- **Format:** PyTorch (no ONNX export yet)
- **sherpa-onnx compatible:** No — no ONNX export available

- **Score:** 4.0/10 (Italian quality: 4 — unproven, Size: 8 — 0.6B same class, On-device: 3 — no ONNX, Multilingual: 7 — includes Italian, Maintenance: 4 — research model, 1 paper)

- **Effort to integrate:** high — would need custom ONNX export and inference pipeline
- **Notes:** Research paper: "Data-Efficient On-Policy Distillation for ASR." The distillation methodology is worth tracking — if it produces better models at 0.6B, it could apply to other architectures.

### Cohere Transcribe ONNX Export (NEW)
- **Source:** https://huggingface.co/christopherthompson81/cohere-transcribe-03-2026-onnx
- **Base:** Cohere Transcribe (2B params, 14 langs including Italian)
- **Size:** ~120 MB encoder (48-layer PE dedup), decoder with KV cache
- **Languages:** ar, de, el, en, es, fr, **it**, ja, ko, nl, pl, pt, vi, zh

- **sherpa-onnx compatible:** No — uses Conformer encoder + Transformer decoder (autoregressive), different from RNNT pipeline
- **Score:** 5.0/10 (Italian quality: 8 — best-in-class WER, Size: 5 — 2B base, On-device: 4 — autoregressive decoder slower than RNNT, Multilingual: 7 — 14 langs, Maintenance: 6 — community export)
- **Recommendation:** **watch** — best-in-class quality with Italian but 2B params and different inference paradigm. If a smaller variant appears or sherpa-onnx adds Transformer-decoder ASR support, this becomes relevant.
- **Effort to integrate:** high — would need new autoregressive decoder backend

## Landscape Developments

**Nemotron-3.5-ASR is released and it changes the calculus for Anti-Vocale.** The combination of streaming capability (progressive display), 40 languages with Italian in top tier, auto language detection, built-in punctuation, and 0.6B parameter size makes it the most complete on-device ASR model available. The soniqo ONNX exports and speech-android SDK provide immediate integration paths. The key question is quality: does it beat Parakeet on real WhatsApp audio? The FLEURS benchmark (5.23 WER Italian with LangID) is slightly worse than our measured Parakeet (~4.3% on clean FLEURS), but streaming mode and auto-detect may offset this in real usage. The INT8 quality regression (+5.6 WER on English) is concerning and must be tested for Italian specifically.

**Gemma 4 QAT Mobile GGUF** is a signal that Google is serious about mobile multimodal deployment. The `any-to-any` pipeline tag and `qat-mobile` source model suggest a deliberate mobile strategy. If llama.cpp adds Gemma 4 audio processing support, these GGUF variants could bypass the current llama-bro blocker. Worth tracking llama.cpp PRs for Gemma 4 audio.

**ARK-ASR-0.6B** introduces "data-efficient on-policy distillation" — a technique that could improve 0.6B model quality without increasing size. While the model itself isn't directly usable (no ONNX), the methodology may influence future Nemotron or Qwen3 distillation efforts.

**Qualcomm NPU acceleration** continues maturing in sherpa-onnx — QNN streaming Zipformer is fully merged with Android demo. While currently targeting English, the infrastructure for multilingual NPU-accelerated ASR is being built.
