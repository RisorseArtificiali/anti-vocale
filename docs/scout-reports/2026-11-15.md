# Model Scout 2026-11-15

**Scope:** frameworks (emphasis) + minor-language model sweep for low WER in today-weak tradition languages (fa he el vi ko zh ar fi). **Baseline:** sherpa-onnx v2.0.1 (our pin, still latest code release), ONNX Runtime 1.31.x-era inside our AAR, LiteRT-LM 0.17.1 ours / 0.19.1 latest. Previous report: 2026-11-11 (headline: Finnish gap-filler, shipped).

## Executive Summary

Frameworks: **sherpa-onnx has NO new code release since v2.0.1** (Oct 26); the only newer artifact is a forced-alignment models repo (Nov 11, not a runtime). ONNX Runtime shipped **v1.32.0 (Nov 13)** plus 1.31.1/1.30.1: quality/infra releases (opset 28, MoE CPU offload, session-init speedups), nothing that changes our Android ARM64 ASR path today, but the session-initialization speedup class is worth watching for our 640-988MB model loads. LiteRT-LM moved to **0.19.x** (0.19.0 Nov 2, 0.19.1 Nov 5) while we sit on 0.17.1; TASK-783 already tracks the hold-and-watch decision.

The minor-language sweep found two real candidates in our weak set, both community fine-tunes of architectures we ALREADY run (zero new runtime work):

1. **Persian/Farsi: mehdi-hf/nemotron-asr-streaming-farsi** with 8.8 WER FLEURS-fa (vs 24.2 for NVIDIA's stock Persian), 600M, FastConformer streaming RNNT; ALREADY pre-converted to sherpa-onnx by mentisialog (Oct 21) AND to LiteRT/NPU by the community (Nov 8). Persian is a tradition language with NO dedicated option today. This is the headline.
2. **Arabic dialectal: oddadmix/nemotron-3.5-asr-arabic-dialectal-v2**, a fine-tune of our exact streaming Nemotron on a 52k-clip dialect-balanced set; OpenVoiceOS ships the ONNX (openmdw license). Dialectal Arabic (our ar-EG axis) is uncovered today.

Non-findings worth recording: csukuangfj's brand-new `audio8-asr-0.1b` (Nov 13) is an EMPTY repo (just .gitattributes); the nabra-82m-arabic is TTS not ASR; Hebrew's ivrit-ai line updated (yi-turbo ct2 Oct 26, zipformer2-66m-streaming-he Oct 14, sherpa-onnx layout INCLUDED in the repo: a ready candidate, 66M, but card WER is unlisted); Greek and Vietnamese ONNX searches returned nothing new; Korean sherpa zipformers are 2024-era (stale).

## Framework Findings

### sherpa-onnx: NO new release (steady)

- Latest code release remains **v2.0.1 (2026-10-26)**, our pin. Since then only `fa-models` (2026-11-11), a forced-alignment model collection, not a runtime. **No action.** The v2.0.1 momentum (hotwords hardening we ride for biasing, the streaming createStream hotwords param) is already in our AAR.

### ONNX Runtime 1.32.0 (2026-11-13): infra release, watch one class

- Source: https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/releases/tag/v1.32.0 (and v1.31.1 Oct 26, v1.30.1 Oct 29)
- Relevant-to-us highlights: opset 28 support; **"dramatically accelerates session initialization on large graphs"** (100k-node graphs: minutes to seconds); CPU QMoE kernels (NVFP4/INT4 block-packed), the INT4-class CPU path our future-quantized models could ride; reliability fixes.
- Android impact today: our models load through sherpa's bundled ORT inside the AAR; we consume ORT only transitively. No action available or needed until sherpa-onnx itself rebases its Android ORT.
- **Watch item:** the session-init speedup + QMoE CPU kernels will matter the day sherpa-onnx adopts 1.32 (our Parakeet load is ~9s on the RMX3853; an ORT-side init win would land there).

### LiteRT-LM 0.19.x: still holding on 0.17.1 (TASK-783 stance unchanged)

- 0.19.0 (2026-11-02) and 0.19.1 (2026-11-05) shipped since our 2026-11-11 report noted the same gap. No changelog line demands a bump for our single-Gemma-at-a-time audio path. **No action; TASK-783 keeps the watch.**

## ASR Model Findings (minor-language sweep)

### mehdi-hf/nemotron-asr-streaming-farsi (HEADLINE: the Persian gap)

- **Source:** https://huggingface.co/mehdi-hf/nemotron-asr-streaming-farsi (base: NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 0.6B streaming, fine-tuned for Persian; author = mallahyari, a NeMo contributor)
- **Pre-converted ALREADY:** sherpa-onnx layout by mentisialog/sherpa-onnx-nemotron-asr-streaming-farsi-1120ms-int8 (Oct 21, https://huggingface.co/mentisialog/sherpa-onnx-nemotron-asr-streaming-farsi-1120ms-int8) and LiteRT/NPU by mlboydaisuke/nemotron-asr-streaming-farsi-LiteRT (Nov 8, 79 downloads)
- **Size:** ~600M params; the int8 sherpa tarball should land near our streaming Nemotron's ~670MB (verify at download)
- **Languages:** fa only. Persian is a tradition language (the Shenava line) with NO dedicated model today: inside the multilingual sets only.
- **Quality (card-published, author-run):** FLEURS-fa **8.81 WER / 2.94 CER** (streaming 1.12s look-ahead) vs **24.2** for NVIDIA's stock Persian; conversational YouTube/film set 26.0 vs 54.4. Cross-source caveat: author numbers; our harness decodes pure (no fusion claimed on the card; the tokenizer is a new 1,024-piece Persian BPE, so the vocab rides the export). Confidence: medium-high (two independent community conversions corroborate usability; numbers unaudited by us).
- **Fit with our stack:** EXACT. It is our streaming Nemotron architecture (FastConformer RNNT, cache-aware streaming, 1.12s look-ahead = our existing tail-pad machinery), so the built-in-style OnlineRecognizer path applies; as a catalog entry it could ride the streaming external lane (TASK-984's streaming manifest handling) or the nemotron-streaming built-in shape.
- **License:** "other" (the NVIDIA Nemotron license base). CHECK the license text before any catalog listing; our Fritter streaming entry already rides the same base, so precedent exists.
- **Score:** 4.3/10 (quality 5 for fa, size 4, on-device 5 proven architecture, coverage 5 a gap language, catalog 4, maintenance 3)
- **Recommendation:** CONVERT-AND-BENCH. Download mentisialog's int8 tarball, run our FLEURS-fa harness leg (the number to beat: our multilingual-set fa quality, unmeasured but weak by every cross-report), then the device streaming leg (RTF + RSS on the RMX3853, the same 1120ms chunk shape we already ship). If our bench lands anywhere near the card's 8.8, this is the Persian model.
- **Effort:** small-medium (pre-converted; catalog entry + streaming validation; license check)

### oddadmix/nemotron-3.5-asr-arabic-dialectal-v2 (dialectal Arabic, the ar-EG axis)

- **Source:** https://huggingface.co/oddadmix/nemotron-3.5-asr-arabic-dialectal-v2 ; ONNX by OpenVoiceOS/nemotron-3.5-asr-arabic-dialectal-v2-onnx (Aug 3, openmdw-1.1)
- **Size:** same 0.6B streaming Nemotron shape; ONNX export exists (onnx-asr library layout, NOT sherpa layout; needs the standard NeMo-to-sherpa streaming export)
- **Languages:** dialectal ar (the fine-tune target: lahgtna-v3-small, 52k clips across Gulf/Egyptian/Levantine/Maghrebi)
- **Quality (card-published):** balanced 2,445-clip dialect test 43.2 WER overall (per-dialect 27-37 for Gulf/Egyptian, Maghrebi hardest); MSA numbers are not the target here, dialectal is. Our current ar coverage: MSA via the multilingual sets, dialectal quality unknown/poor (the ar-EG axis is a tradition interest). Confidence: low-medium (author numbers; dialect WER is intrinsically high across every system; the 27-37 band for the big dialects is competitive for the class).
- **Fit:** our exact streaming architecture again; the ONNX is onnx-asr not sherpa, so the conversion is the standard streaming export (the Fritter streaming precedent).
- **License:** openmdw-1.1 (permissive, attribution-style); catalog-compatible.
- **Score:** 3.4/10 (quality 3 for dialectal ar, size 4, on-device 5, coverage 4 the dialect axis, catalog 4, maintenance 3)
- **Recommendation:** watch-list first, bench second. The v2 card is honest that Maghrebi is hardest; our ar users are ar-EG-heavy and the Egyptian band (29.1 WER) is the interesting one. Behind the Farsi candidate because dialect WER is high in absolute terms.
- **Effort:** medium (streaming conversion + a dialectal eval set, which we lack; the FLEURS ar leg is MSA)

### ivrit-ai/zipformer2-66m-streaming-he (Hebrew, ready-but-unproven)

- **Source:** https://huggingface.co/ivrit-ai/zipformer2-66m-streaming-he (mirror of thewh1teagle's model; CC-BY-4.0; sherpa-onnx layout INCLUDED in-repo; used by live-transcribe.ivrit.ai in production)
- **Size:** 66M params, tiny (tens of MB int8)
- **Languages:** he only. Tradition language (the ivrit-ai line) with no dedicated option today.
- **Quality:** NO published WER on the card (production use by ivrit.ai's live transcriber is the signal); the OpenVoiceOS ivrit-whisper-large-v3 line is the quality alternative at 10-20x the size. Confidence: low (unverified; a 66M fine-tune from a LibriSpeech English base plausibly lands mid-tier on clean speech, poor on noise).
- **Fit:** sherpa-onnx streaming zipformer. Our CTC/zipformer external lane handles NON-streaming zipformers; a STREAMING zipformer is not one of our seven import families. Would need either a family addition or an offline re-export (zipformer transducers can decode offline).
- **Score:** 3.0/10 (quality 2 unproven, size 5, on-device 5, coverage 4 gap language, catalog 2 family gap, maintenance 4)
- **Recommendation:** cheap to BENCH (sherpa files in-repo; our FLEURS-he leg exists in the harness): one desktop leg tells us if a 66M Hebrew model beats the multilingual sets. Only then decide the import-lane question.
- **Effort:** small for the bench; the import lane is the open question.
