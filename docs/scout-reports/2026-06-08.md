# Model Scout Report — 2026-06-08

## Executive Summary

Short cycle (2 days since last report). **One major integration milestone**: Cohere Transcribe is now natively supported in sherpa-onnx (PR #3456 merged), adding a 2B-param ASR model with Italian support directly into Anti-Vocale's inference framework. **One quality warning**: the official ONNX INT4 Nemotron 3.5 export shows Italian WER degrading to 6.69% (vs 4.25% at full precision) — INT4 is too aggressive for Nemotron. **One new model to watch**: Gemma 4 12B Unified via LiteRT-LM achieves the best audio FLEURS score of any Gemma variant (0.069 vs 0.08–0.09) but is 6.2 GB and desktop-only. ### onnx-community Nemotron 3.5 ASR INT4 — Italian Quality Degradation (NEW)

- **Source:** https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/nemotron-3.5-asr-streaming-0.6b-onnx-int4
- **Created:** June 4, 2026 | **Last modified:** June 8, 2026
- **Downloads:** 1,013
- **Format:** ONNX INT4 (onnxruntime-genai)
- **Languages:** 40 including Italian (it-IT) in top tier
- **Architecture:** Cache-Aware FastConformer-RNNT, 560ms chunk optimized
- **Italian WER on FLEURS:**
  - Full precision (NeMo): 4.25% (LangID, 1.12s chunk)
  - **INT4 ONNX: 6.69%** (at 560ms chunk) — **56% degradation**
- **Score:** 6/10 (Italian quality: 5 — 6.69% WER is worse than Parakeet (5.4%), Size: 8 — INT4 is very compact, On-device: 5 — requires onnxruntime-genai, not sherpa-onnx, Multilingual: 9 — 40 langs, Maintenance: 7 — official Microsoft export)
- **Recommendation:** **AVOID INT4 for Italian** — The 56% quality degradation makes INT4 uncompetitive. If pursuing Nemotron, need INT8 or FP16 ONNX export. The soniqo INT8 ONNX export (~720 MB) is the better candidate despite having unknown Italian INT8 quality.
- **Notes:** This is the official Microsoft ONNX export. The quality gap is likely due to INT4 being too aggressive for the FastConformer-RNNT architecture, combined with the shorter 560ms chunk size vs the full-precision 1.12s chunk.

## Landscape Developments

**Cohere Transcribe entering sherpa-onnx** is the most impactful landscape change this cycle. It provides a new Italian-capable ASR backend without requiring a new inference framework. The model is 2B params (large) but community INT4 at 1.94 GB makes it potentially manageable. Italian WER is unknown and must be benchmarked. The model sits between specialized (Distil IT at 938 MB, 14.3% WER-equivalent) and general-purpose (Parakeet at 464 MB, 5.4% WER) in terms of architecture philosophy.

**Nemotron 3.5 INT4 quality gap** confirms that aggressive quantization doesn't work for all architectures. The 56% Italian WER degradation (4.25% → 6.69%) from INT4 quantization of the FastConformer-RNNT is a cautionary finding. For Nemotron integration, INT8 or FP16 is the minimum acceptable quantization level.

**Parakeet ecosystem expansion** continues rapidly. parakeet.cpp GGUF exports demonstrate zero quality loss at Q8_0 (byte-for-byte identical to NeMo), validating that aggressive quantization works well for Parakeet's architecture. CrispASR claims 9.5x speedup over ONNX but is desktop-only. The ecosystem momentum suggests Parakeet will remain the default choice for on-device ASR in the near term.

**Gemma 4 12B Unified** represents the future direction — encoder-free multimodal where raw audio is projected directly into the LLM. At FLEURS 0.069 it's the best Gemma audio performer, but 6.2 GB is impractical for phones. Watch for smaller variants or compression techniques.
