## New Models (since 2026-05-24)

### AlinClaudiu/SpeD-ParakeetRo-110M-onnx
- **Source:** https://huggingface.co/AlinClaudiu/SpeD-ParakeetRo-110M-onnx
- **Size:** ~110M params (small)
- **Languages:** Romanian only
- **Last updated:** 2026-05-25
- **Format:** ONNX
- **sherpa-onnx compatible:** Uncertain — custom NeMo CTC export
- **Score:** 3/10 (Italian quality: 1, Size: 9, On-device: 7, Multilingual: 1, Maintenance: 4)
- **Recommendation:** **watch** — demonstrates Parakeet adaptation to a new Romance language via ONNX. If the same approach were applied to Italian training data, it could produce a small, efficient Italian-specific Parakeet model.
- **Notes:** 29 downloads. Uses FastConformer CTC 110M architecture. The training methodology (if published) would be valuable for Italian adaptation.

### tribber93/whisper-base-onnx-int8-qdq
- **Source:** https://huggingface.co/tribber93/whisper-base-onnx-int8-qdq
- **Size:** Whisper base (~290MB)
- **Languages:** 99 languages including Italian
- **Last updated:** 2026-05-24
- **Format:** ONNX int8 QDQ (Quantization-Aware Design with Quantize/Dequantize)
- **sherpa-onnx compatible:** Potentially — standard Whisper ONNX layout
- **Score:** 3/10 (Italian quality: 3, Size: 8, On-device: 7, Multilingual: 9, Maintenance: 3)
- **Recommendation:** **skip** — Whisper base quality is far below our distil-large-v3-it. QDQ quantization is interesting as a technique but doesn't compensate for the smaller model's lower accuracy.
