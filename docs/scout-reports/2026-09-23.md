## Landscape Developments

Meta's Omnilingual ASR (Dec 2025, 7B LLM-ASR tier claiming SOTA CER across 1600+ languages) has reached the mobile-relevant tier: a 300M CTC int8 with sherpa docs support, plus community 1B conversions. The mass-coverage play for a global app is now a repackaging task, not a research task. Moondream's Parakeet generation refresh (Ultra post-train, Redux ternary 178MB "113x speed on CPU" claims circulating) signals the small-model frontier moving from int8 to ternary; ONNX Runtime has no ternary kernel story yet, which is the adoption gate.
