## Headline: the Parakeet Ultra verdict is now DECISIVE (TASK-823)

A THIRD independent sherpa int8 conversion of moondream's parakeet-ultra appeared on 2026-10-13: **leogaudin/sherpa-onnx-nemo-parakeet-ultra-int8** (652MB encoder, nemo_transducer layout, export script included). TASK-823's original NO-GO carried one caveat: the two prior artifacts came from different converters, so a subtly worse quantization could not be separated from worse weights.

The retest ran this morning on the standing 11-clip Italian set, same harness, same recognizer construction:

| Backend | mean WER | mean CER |
|---|---|---|
| Parakeet v3 (pinned built-in) | 0.031 | 0.014 |
| Ultra int8 (Alexqwqw, TASK-823) | 0.062 | 0.033 |
| **Ultra int8 (leogaudin, this run)** | **0.062** | **0.033** |

Two INDEPENDENT converters produce the IDENTICAL regression to three decimal places. The converter confound is now effectively ruled out: the regression is in the WEIGHTS (moondream's post-training), not the quantization. **Verdict upgraded from NO-GO-with-caveat to NO-GO-confirmed** for Ultra as a v3 replacement on our domain. The watch item narrows to curiosity only: a csukuangfj conversion would add nothing material. Artifacts: `eval/results/summary_20261018-075817.md`; the harness gained the `parakeet_ultra_leogaudin` backend entry.
