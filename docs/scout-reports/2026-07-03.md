### jeffpeng3 Nemotron onnx-int4-dynamic — watch (detail confirmed)
- **Source:** https://huggingface.co/jeffpeng3/nemotron-3.5-asr-streaming-0.6b-onnx-int4-dynamic
- **Size:** INT4 (MatMulNBits, block=128) — est. ~350–400 MB (vs our 640 MB int8)
- **Languages:** 40 language-locales incl. Italian `it` (card body; header auto-tag says "35")
- **Format:** ONNX INT4, **single dynamic encoder** for all chunk sizes (collapses our 80/160/560/1120ms four-variant set into one file) + shared decoder/joint
- **sherpa-onnx compatible:** **NO** — `genai_config.json` layout with cache-aware I/O (`cache_last_channel` / `cache_last_time`), not the canonical sherpa OnlineRecognizer layout. Would need a custom adapter.
- **Downloads:** 378
- **Composite:** 6/10. **Recommendation: Watch.** The single-dynamic-encoder design is genuinely attractive (one model, any chunk size), and it's the most size-aggressive Nemotron. But the genai_config layout blocks drop-in. A *sherpa-native* int4 from csukuangfj would be the real unlock; doesn't exist yet.
