## Executive Summary

Short cycle (2 days since last report). **Nemotron-3.5-ASR Streaming Multilingual is imminently releasing** — NVIDIA confirmed "in the next couple of days" (June 2 comment) with 29 high-quality languages including Italian, plus 11 additional tokenizer-supported languages. ### jinhwan000/qwen3-asr-1.7b-onnx and qwen3-asr-0.6b-onnx (NEW)
- **Source (1.7B):** https://huggingface.co/jinhwan000/qwen3-asr-1.7b-onnx
- **Source (0.6B):** https://huggingface.co/jinhwan000/qwen3-asr-0.6b-onnx
- **Size:** Unknown (complex multi-file structure with external .data files)
- **Languages:** 52 languages including Italian (Qwen3-ASR family)
- **Last updated:** 2026-06-03
- **Downloads:** 69 (1.7B), 66 (0.6B)
- **Format:** ONNX int4 (MatMulNBits) + fp16/fp32 encoder variants. Custom inference loop with separate decoder graphs: `decoder_init`, `decoder_prefill`, `decoder_step` + bucket variants for CUDA.
- **sherpa-onnx compatible:** **No** — these are raw ONNX Runtime exports with a custom C#/Python inference pipeline, not sherpa-onnx format. No tokens.txt, no sherpa-onnx config. Uses `embed_tokens.bin` for raw embedding lookup.
- **Italian quality assessment:** Unbenchmarked. The 0.6B Qwen3-ASR scored 12.2% WER in our benchmark vs 4.3% for Distil IT. The 1.7B variant should be significantly better but is much larger.
- **Score:** 4.5/10 (Italian quality: 5 — unproven, Size: 4 — multi-GB, On-device: 5 — not sherpa-onnx compatible, Multilingual: 8 — 52 langs, Maintenance: 5 — experimental PoC)
- **Recommendation:** **skip for now** — not sherpa-onnx compatible. ilmina/qwen3-asr-1.7b-sherpa-onnx remains the better target for our pipeline. However, the int4 quantization and bucket decoder approach are interesting for future optimization.
- **Effort to integrate:** high — would need custom ONNX Runtime inference pipeline
- **Notes:** Companion forced aligner also available: `jinhwan000/qwen3-aligner-0.6b-onnx`. The detailed README with full Python inference code is a valuable reference for anyone building a custom Qwen3-ASR pipeline.

### lwittich/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3-onnx-int8-cpu (NEW)
- **Source:** https://huggingface.co/lwittich/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3-onnx-int8-cpu
- **Size:** Unknown (encoder + decoder_joint, both with external .data)
- **Languages:** 25 European languages (same as our Parakeet v3)
- **Last updated:** 2026-06-04
- **Format:** ONNX INT8 (selective: MatMul/Gemm quantized, Conv in FP32, activations FP32)
- **sherpa-onnx compatible:** Potentially — uses `onnx-asr` library, structure (encoder-model.onnx + decoder_joint-model.onnx + vocab.txt) differs from csukuangfj's sherpa-onnx packaging (encoder-epoch-*.onnx + decoder-epoch-*.onnx + joiner-epoch-*.onnx). May need renaming/repackaging.
- **Benchmark (provided by author):**
  - WER: 6.8% on FLEURS test (25 languages)
  - Median RTF: 0.031 (31x realtime)
  - p95 RTF: 0.039
  - CPU: 4 cores, 8 GiB, CPUExecutionProvider
- **Italian quality assessment:** 6.8% average WER across 25 languages. Italian likely similar to our measured ~4.3% on clean FLEURS. The selective INT8 approach preserves Conv quality while reducing MatMul overhead.
- **Score:** 6.0/10 (Italian quality: 7 — same base model, Size: 7 — presumably similar to 464MB, On-device: 7 — ONNX INT8 but not sherpa-onnx packaged, Multilingual: 7 — 25 langs, Maintenance: 5 — community export)
- **Recommendation:** **watch** — benchmark data is useful for comparison. The selective INT8 approach (MatMul/Gemm only) is a quantization strategy worth noting for our own model optimization. Not actionable until confirmed sherpa-onnx compatible.
- **Effort to integrate:** medium — may need repackaging or adapter
- **Notes:** Uses `onnx-asr` library (pip install onnx-asr). The quantization rationale (Conv quantization increased overhead and degraded quality) is an important data point for our own ONNX quantization efforts.

## Landscape Developments

**Nemotron-3.5-ASR Streaming Multilingual GA is imminent.** NVIDIA's June 2 comment ("in the next couple of days") is the strongest signal yet. The confirmed 40-language support (29 high-quality + 11 tokenizer-supported) with Italian in the top tier makes this the most consequential model release for Anti-Vocale since Parakeet TDT v3. The streaming architecture enables progressive display — directly addressing the limitation identified in our VAD+Whisper segmentation research. Combined with fine-tuning recipes ($50 per language on L40S), this could become a customizable, always-improving ASR backbone.

**Qualcomm NPU acceleration for ASR** is rapidly maturing. Both sherpa-onnx (QNN streaming Zipformer) and Qualcomm AI Hub (Distil-Whisper for Snapdragon) are producing NPU-optimized ASR models. While currently English-focused, this establishes the deployment pattern for future multilingual models on Qualcomm devices — relevant given Anti-Vocale runs on a Realme (Snapdragon) device.

**Gemma 4 in LiteRT-LM now covers E2B through 12B** (270M to 6.1 GB), with audio modality confirmed across the range. The 12B at 6.1 GB is too large for current phones but demonstrates Google's commitment to edge LLM deployment. The E2B and E4B models remain the practical choices for on-device audio transcription.
