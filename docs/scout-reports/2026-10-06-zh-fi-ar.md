## Appendix (same day, evening): local Egyptian horserace + true v2 conversion

The dmouayad "v2" sherpa package is byte-identical to our v1 (encoder sha256 b3343d6d...: they repackaged our files), so a true v2 was converted locally through the community-model-conversion pipeline (transformers-5.x naming updates in the remapper; manual safetensors reader; learned positional embeddings copied; dynamo=False decoder export on torch 2.14; per-channel int8 decoder). Conversion validated three ways: .pt decode, sherpa decode, and a 71s tiled decode with no EOT truncation.

Verdict on the 35-clean-clip Egyptian sample (our harness, our int8 for BOTH arms):

| arm | WER | note |
|---|---|---|
| v1 dialectal (catalog today) | 0.453 | baseline |
| v2 dialectal (our conversion) | 0.489 | bootstrap v2-v1: +0.036, CI95 [-0.003, +0.091], v2 wins 6/35 clips |
| whisper-small stock | 0.632 | multilingual baseline |

The v2 does NOT beat the v1 in the apples-to-apples (both our conversions, same harness/normalization), against the oddadmix card's Egyptian row (0.245 on their full 200-clip test with their clean_text normalization and their own exports). Both numbers are honest; they measure differently. For the catalog decision the local comparison governs: NO-GO on replacing v1. Options left open: the small v2 (their Egyptian 0.340 at ~250MB, needs conversion) and asking oddadmix about the gap.

## Appendix 2: the small v2, and the Hebrew-fix impact check

The small v2 (our conversion, same pipeline: 80 mels, 12+12 layers, per-channel int8 decoder) lands at 358MB total and 0.486 WER on the same 35-clip Egyptian sample: statistically even with the full turbo v2 (0.489), 3 points behind our v1 (0.453), and far ahead of the stock whisper-small (0.632). Tiled 71s decodes 63 words with no EOT truncation. The interesting catalog shape may be ADD-light rather than REPLACE: keep the v1 turbo as the quality pick and add the small v2 as the light option (a third of the download for ~3 WER points).

The upstream Hebrew contribution (sherpa-onnx #3964/#3965, the byte-level-BPE character-loss fix, merged 2026-09-20) is untouched by today's work: our patches live only in scripts/whisper/export-onnx.py (build-time), the fix in the runtime C++. Empirically the Arabic vocab on the pinned 1.13.8 shows no character mangling (sherpa-vs-.pt transcripts are character-identical on real-length clips; short-clip differences are decode-path variance, not the bug's signature). v1.13.9 is not published yet, so the fix is not consumable today; when the tag lands, TASK-668 bumps the pin and this harness re-runs in minutes to confirm the absolutes.
