# Model Scout Report — 2026-05-20

## Executive Summary

8 new models/frameworks discovered since last scout (2026-05-17), most significantly **Cohere Transcribe** — a new 2B-param ASR model with full sherpa-onnx int8 integration and Italian support among 14 languages. LiteRT-LM v0.12.0 was released (we're on v0.11.0). The Gemma 4 E2B litertlm ecosystem continues to grow with audio encoder support confirmed via CoreML exports. No new Italian-dedicated models found — our Distil-Large-V3-IT remains best-in-class for Italian on-device ASR.

## ASR Model Findings

### Cohere Transcribe (CohereLabs/cohere-transcribe-03-2026)
- **Source:** https://huggingface.co/CohereLabs/cohere-transcribe-03-2026
- **Size:** ~1.6 GB int8 (2B params)
- **Languages:** 14 — ar, de, el, en, es, fr, **it**, ja, ko, nl, pl, pt, vi, zh
- **Last updated:** 2026-05-04
- **Format:** ONNX int8, sherpa-onnx compatible (integrated in v1.12.35, April 2026)
- **sherpa-onnx compatible:** Yes — full integration across all bindings (Java/Kotlin, C#, Go, etc.)
- **sherpa-onnx export:** `csukuangfj2/sherpa-onnx-cohere-transcribe-14-lang-int8-2026-04-01`
- **Italian quality assessment:** Not independently benchmarked for Italian. 946 likes, 278K downloads on HuggingFace suggest strong community adoption. Purpose-built for transcription (not a general LLM).
- **Score:** 7.5/10 (Italian quality: 7, Size: 5, On-device: 9, Multilingual: 6, Maintenance: 9)
- **Recommendation:** **evaluate** — benchmark Italian WER against Distil-IT and Parakeet v3. At 1.6GB it's larger than our current ASR models but the quality could justify it.
- **Effort to integrate:** low — sherpa-onnx already supports it, just need model download config and backend wiring
- **Notes:** First non-Whisper/non-Parakeet ASR with Italian support in the sherpa-onnx ecosystem. Apache 2.0 license. If Italian quality is competitive, this could replace Whisper Turbo (988MB) or Medium (903MB) for multilingual use.

### Gemma 4 E2B litertlm ecosystem
- **Sources:** Multiple — `Jobnow/gemma-4-E2B-it.litertlm` (2026-05-16, MIT), `PeppX/gemma-4-e2b-uncensored-litertlm` (2026-05-20), `vinay7525/Gemma4_e2b_it_litertlm` (2026-05-20), `Sidharth1743/gemma-4-E2B-it-litertlm` (2026-05-10)
- **Size:** ~1.3 GB quantized
- **Languages:** 140+
- **Format:** .litertlm (compatible with our `LlmTranscriptionBackend`)
- **Audio support:** CONFIRMED — CoreML export (`aoiandroid/gemma-4-E2B-coreml`, 2026-05-20) includes dedicated `audio.mlmodelc` encoder, confirming E2B has native audio capabilities. Our LiteRT-LM backend should support this.
- **Score:** 7/10 (Italian quality: 8, Size: 9 — 1.3GB is very compact, On-device: 7, Multilingual: 9, Maintenance: 5 — community conversions)
- **Recommendation:** **evaluate** — test `Jobnow/gemma-4-E2B-it.litertlm` for Italian audio transcription. If quality is acceptable, this would be a much smaller alternative to our 4.9GB E4B model.
- **Effort to integrate:** low — our LiteRT-LM backend already exists, just need to add E2B model download option
- **Notes:** E2B uses Per-Layer Embeddings (PLE) to deliver ~5B model quality at ~1.3GB. All community conversions have 0 downloads. The `Jobnow` variant (MIT license) looks cleanest for initial testing.

## Landscape Developments

**Cohere Transcribe integration in sherpa-onnx:** The most significant landscape change since last scout. Cohere Transcribe is now fully supported in sherpa-onnx (since v1.12.35, April 2026) with int8 quantized exports across all language bindings. This is the first non-Whisper/non-Parakeet ASR model with Italian support in the sherpa-onnx ecosystem, and it's purpose-built for transcription rather than being a general speech model.

**Qualcomm NPU ASR push:** Qualcomm published official Whisper and Distil-Whisper models for their AI Hub with pre-compiled NPU binaries achieving ~258-590ms encoder inference on Snapdragon chipsets. This signals hardware-accelerated on-device ASR becoming mainstream for Snapdragon devices. Not applicable to our MediaTek device, but relevant if we expand device targeting.

**Gemma 4 E2B audio confirmation:** The CoreML export of Gemma 4 E2B (`aoiandroid/gemma-4-E2B-coreml`) includes a dedicated `audio.mlmodelc` encoder, confirming that the E2B model architecture supports audio input natively. Combined with the growing litertlm conversion ecosystem, this suggests the E2B litertlm audio path should work through our existing LiteRT-LM backend.

**No new Italian-dedicated models:** The Italian ASR landscape remains unchanged. No new Whisper Italian fine-tunes, no Italian Parakeet variants. Our Distil-Large-V3-IT (4.3% WER) remains the best Italian-specific model.
