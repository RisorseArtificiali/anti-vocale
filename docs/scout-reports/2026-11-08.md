## Finding 1: Parakeet Ultra sherpa-onnx int8 (HEADLINE)

**Source:** [csukuangfj/sherpa-onnx-nemo-parakeet-tdt-0.6b-ultra-int8](https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/sherpa-onnx-nemo-parakeet-tdt-0.6b-ultra-int8) (int8) and [csukuangfj/sherpa-onnx-nemo-parakeet-tdt-0.6b-ultra](https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/sherpa-onnx-nemo-parakeet-tdt-0.6b-ultra) (fp32), both published 2026-11-06/07. Base model: [moondream/parakeet-ultra](https://huggingface.co/moondream/parakeet-ultra) (129 likes, 53809 downloads, CC-BY-4.0, Open ASR Leaderboard eval results on the card).

**What it is:** a TDT 0.6b transducer (same architecture as our Parakeet v3 default) trained by the moondream org, covering the same 25 EU languages as v3 (en, de, fr, es, it, pt, ru, uk, hr, sl, lv, lt, et, fi, sv, da, nl, pl, cs, sk, hu, ro, bg, el, mt). Already pre-converted for sherpa-onnx by csukuangfj with both fp32 and int8 quantization.

**Why it matters:** this is the model the 2026-10-13 scout flagged as a headline; the sherpa-onnx export is the piece that was missing. It is a drop-in catalog candidate: the Transducer family path is proven, the files are already sherpa-shaped, and the community catalog reaches users without an app release. The key question is whether it beats our current Parakeet v3 stock-int8 on the quality matrix; the Open ASR Leaderboard results on the model card suggest it is competitive, but our own bench is the gate.

**Scores:**

| Criterion | Score | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Tradition-language quality | 4 | Same 25 EU languages as v3; published leaderboard results; our bench needed to confirm |
| Size efficiency | 4 | Same architecture/size class as v3 (0.6b params); int8 expected ~640MB |

| Community-catalog importability | 5 | Transducer family, pre-converted, no conversion work needed |
| Maintenance health | 4 | Fresh export (Nov 6); moondream org active |

**Composite: 4.1/10** (quality is the unknown; if the bench confirms a WER improvement over v3, this jumps to 5+).

**Import path:** community-model-conversion NOT needed (already sherpa-shaped). Direct catalog entry from the csukuangfj repo (same pattern as the existing Parakeet stock-int8 and all zipformer entries). Device-validation pass required per the standing rule. Then bench on FLEURS-40 per language (the eval harness handles this today).

**Recommendation:** bench-first (TASK: bench Parakeet Ultra int8 on FLEURS it/en vs current Parakeet v3 stock-int8 and turbo). If it wins or ties on quality at the same speed, catalog it as an alternative variant alongside stock-int8 and smoothquant.

## Finding 3: Nemotron streaming quantization paper

**Source:** [arxiv.org/pdf/2604.14493](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.14493) (April 2026, "Pushing the Limits of On-Device Streaming ASR").

**What it is:** a systematic study of on-device streaming ASR quantization, identifying Nemotron Speech Streaming as the strongest candidate, achieving 0.67GB int4 with WER within 1% of full precision. Relevant to our streaming architecture (we ship the 1.6GB int8 Nemotron): the int4 quantization could halve the streaming model's size if sherpa-onnx adds k-quant support.

**Action:** none today (sherpa-onnx has no k-quant); watch for sherpa-onnx to add it.
