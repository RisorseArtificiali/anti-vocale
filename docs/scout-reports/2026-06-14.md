**Assessment:**
- **jeffpeng3's int4 experiments** are the most size-relevant direction (~340 MB vs int8's 682 MB vs fp32's 2.5 GB) but are bare repos with no documentation, no downloads, "multi-encoder"/"self-contained" non-standard naming, and unproven int4 ASR quality. Not actionable without significant validation work.
- **cstr's GGUF export** (110 downloads, 3 days) confirms strong ecosystem interest in Nemotron 3.5 streaming, but GGUF ASR inference on Android is not a viable path (no runtime).
- **No new sherpa-onnx-format Nemotron variants.** csukuangfj2's int8 export (June 12) remains the only sherpa-onnx-native option beyond Anti-Vocale's existing fp32.
